Hi, I'm Polina, and this is a plot of digital land I staked out for myself. Here, I write about my projects, hobbies, or whatever is on my mind. 

I studied [Engineering Physics at UBC](https://projectlab.engphys.ubc.ca/) and now work as a software engineer in Vancouver, building high-performance C++ software for machine vision [cameras](https://emergentvisiontec.com/). I’m interested in computer vision, machine learning, and robotics, and am always learning something new. Outside of tech, I enjoy cooking, travelling, and photography.

This website is called *issledovatel*, pronounced [ɪs⁽ʲ⁾ːˈlʲedəvətʲɪlʲ], which means *explorer* in Russian. I got this domain because I wanted to self-host a SearXNG search engine instance and thought the play on *Internet Explorer* was very clever. Needless to say, I did not end up using the domain for its intended purpose, but instead used it for my personal site.
